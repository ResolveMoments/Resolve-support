Privacy & Data Protection

How We Protect Your Privacy

Your privacy is our top priority. We've designed this app to keep your personal content completely private while providing essential account functionality.

🔒 Local Storage (Your Personal Content)

The following are stored on your device only and never sent to our or any 3rd party servers:

• Your health conditions
• Your thoughts
• Your notes
• Your ratings
• Your journals
• Any other personal content
📧 Server Storage (Account Management)

The following are stored on our servers for functionality, legal, and security reasons:

• Your email address (for login and account recovery)
• Encrypted password hash (for authentication)
• Birthday (to confirm you are 18 or older)
• Subscription records (plan, purchase and renewal dates, and Apple transaction identifiers). We never receive your payment details.
• IP address, device, and account creation/login timestamps by our hosting provider for security but not collected nor used (except account creation timestamps) by Resolve

Privacy Benefits

Storing content locally ensures that only those who can access your account through your physical device have access to your personal content. This means:

• Resolve Moments, LLC cannot access your personal content
• Governmental entities cannot access your personal content
• Third party companies cannot access your personal content
• Note this assumes you keep your personal content on your device only and do not send it elsewhere (for example: your iCloud) nor give your device to any 3rd parties.

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

• Access all your stored personal data anytime
• Delete your account along with your personal data anytime
• Delete your personal content while maintaining your account at anytime
• Manage your account preferences
This privacy first approach ensures your mental health journey remains completely private while providing essential account functionality.
